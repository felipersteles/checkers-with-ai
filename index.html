  • Control the center of the board early on.
  • Keep your back row intact to prevent your opponent from getting kings.
  • Move in groups — lone pieces are easy targets.
  • Force your opponent into double jumps you can block.
  • Trade pieces when you're ahead; avoid trades when behind.
  • Green squares show where a selected piece can move.
  • Each player starts with 12 pieces on dark squares.
  • White starts.
  • Pieces move diagonally forward one square at a time.
  • Captures are made by jumping over an enemy piece to an empty square.
  • Captures are mandatory when available.
  • Multiple captures in one turn are allowed and encouraged.
  • Men cannot capture Kings.
  • When multiple captures are available, the longest capture is mandatory.
  • Reach the opponent's back row to become a King.
  • Kings can move and capture both forward and backward.
  • The player who captures all enemy pieces wins.
  • If a player has no moves left, the player with more pieces wins.
  • Click a piece to select it.
  • Green squares highlight valid moves.
  • Click a green square to move there.
  • Turns alternate between White and Black.
  • Press New Game to reset the board.
